# Gusto Maina
## 2026 Oil on Canvas 14” x 18”

This is a street in Old Plovdiv, Bulgaria. The city of my birthplace. “Guso Maina” is a colloquial phrase specific to my city which roughly translates to “Enjoy it!”. I started this painting back in 1998. At the time it seemed too ambitious and complex for me and I never finished it. It was the first painting I completed in December of 2025 since I stopped painting as a kid. It was a test project to get my bearings as well as see if my paints and brushes were still in good shape. I added my children in it since we had visited that location earlier in the year. When I started this painting I never envisioned that I would finish it 27 years later with my kids in it.
