# The Miller House at 8:26 AM, October 29, 1776
## 2026 Oil on Canvas 22” x 32”

A hand crafted custom frame frames this absurdly specific and detailed scene, depicting George Washington at the Elijah Miller House. The painting was a special project for the North Castle 250th Anniversary Art Contest and was awarded first prize.
Depicted are historically significant figures of the American Revolution that were all believed to be present at the Elijah Miller House on that day, including the dogs. The Miller House served as Washington’s headquarters after the Battle of White Plains and as a field hospital throughout the revolution. The Miller House is preserved and maintained by the Daughters of Liberty and is a landmark in Westchester, New York.
4600 North Castle residents received a bookmark from the Historical Society featuring the painting. Gifted to the North Castle Historical Society Permanent Collection.
