# Kon Seré
## 2026 Oil on Canvas 26” x 20”

Horse on a toilet, commissioned by my 6 year old son. The title is the phonetic pronunciation in Bulgarian for a horse “sending a fax”, just to make it sound fancy.
A great deal of horse anatomy was studied to ensure the pose is physically correct.
The newspaper “The Stable Times” features a cover story about another horse who has run into some trouble doing the same exact thing, potentially giving the horse a cause for concern. Interpreting the painting as commentary on the amount of time wasted doom scrolling social media while taking care of personal business is acceptable.
