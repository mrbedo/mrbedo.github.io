# Almost
## 2026 Oil on Canvas 40” x 64”

This painting is about failing to complete goals. The red racetrack and the yellow #40 Porsche which is inevitably going to win the race, depicted in primary colors, represent two constants; The world we navigate and time. The number 40 has often been used throughout history to represent periods of trial and endurance. In contrast, the #6 Mercedes, which isn't going to win the race, represents the flaws of man. It is not in a primary color. Perhaps, almost. Ultimately, in a race against time, no one ever crosses the finish line, because if nothing gets you in the end, time will.
